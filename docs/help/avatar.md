# Block Avatar

Block Avatar is a desktop app that makes your block-style character move with your voice.
It is built for streaming through OBS. It lip-syncs to your microphone, reacts to how loud you
are and plays gestures and emotes.

It uses the same characters and blocks as Vox. For the full manual, including its keys and its
chat and viewer features, see:

[Block Avatar manual](../avatar/manual.md)

# Voice

Voice chat lets you talk to players near you in a world, or to your raid party wherever they
are in that world. It is part of the desktop client. It is not in the browser yet.

## What it is

- **Nearby** voice reaches players close to you. It gets quieter with distance and fades out.
- **Party** voice reaches members of your raid party in the same world, at any distance.

## Turning it on

Voice is off by default. Nothing opens your microphone or speakers until you turn it on.

1. Press Esc to open the menu, then choose **Voice**.
2. Choose **Enable voice** so it says **On**.

Your choices are saved, so voice stays as you left it. You can pick the input and output
devices on the same screen, and set the input and output volumes.

## Talking

- **Push to talk** is on by default. Hold the push-to-talk button to send. The default is B on
  the keyboard and the controller's D-pad right. You can change it in the controls menu.
- **Open mic** keeps the microphone open and sends your speech; a silence gate keeps quiet
  out. Change the mode in the Voice screen, under **Talk mode**.
- **Switch channel** with N on the keyboard. The Voice screen's **Channel** row does the same.
  Controllers have no channel button by default.
- **Who hears you:** on the nearby channel, players within 32 blocks hear you by default. Each
  world's server can set its own distance, and your game uses the same one. Your voice is loudest close by, fades as they move away, and sits
  left or right of you by where they stand. On the party channel, members of your raid party
  hear you at any distance.
- Guests have no raid party, so they can use the nearby channel only.

## Hearing others

- **Mute a player:** on the Voice screen, each other player has a **Mute** row. Press it to
  mute or hear that player again (the screen lists up to 12 players). Mutes are not saved, so they do not carry over to your next visit.
- **Volume:** the output volume turns down every voice you hear.
- **Who is speaking:** while you send, a **MIC** marker in the bottom-left corner shows your
  channel (Nearby or Party). Under it, the names of players who are speaking are listed.
- **Mouths move** while a player talks, so you can see who is speaking.

## Privacy

- The server does not record or store voice. It passes each voice on to the players who
  should hear it, and to no one else.
- Your microphone is only opened after you turn voice on.
- While voice is on, your microphone is open and your operating system may show a microphone
  indicator. With push to talk on, nothing is sent unless you hold the button; with open mic,
  the silence gate decides what is sent. Turn **Enable voice** off to close the microphone.

## The browser

Voice is not available in the browser yet. The Voice screen says so there.

# Building

You build with blocks. Each block is a cube, a quarter of a metre (0.25 m) on a side.

## Breaking and placing

- **Left mouse** (hold) breaks the block you aim at.
- **Right mouse** places the block in your hand where you point.
- On a controller, the right trigger breaks and the left trigger places.

## The hotbar

Your hotbar has nine slots. The block in your hand is the one in the selected slot.

- Press **1** to **9** on the keyboard to choose a slot.
- On a controller, the bumpers move to the next and previous slot.

## The quick wheel

The quick wheel shows your nine hotbar blocks in a circle.

1. Hold **Tab** (or the controller's left d-pad button) to open it.
2. Point with the mouse or the right stick. The top slot is first, then the rest go clockwise.
3. Let go to take the block you point at.

## Picking a block

Pick puts the block you aim at into your hand.

- Keyboard and mouse: **middle mouse button**.
- Controller: the **West** button.

## Blocks

Every block is 0.25 m. These are the blocks in the game:

grass, dirt, stone, cobblestone, bricks, planks, log, sand, gravel, snow, leaves, glass,
concrete, metal, tile, lamp, water, obsidian, basalt, magma, ash, crystal, fire, pearl, lumen

## Where you can build

A **plot** is a square of 64 × 64 blocks (16 × 16 m). You claim one for 20 authority, and you
can hold up to six in each world. Only you, or a group you share it with, can build on it.

# Accounts and your character

Open the vox.fail page and sign in. You can use any of these:

- **Sign in with Steam** or **Sign in with Discord**, when the site shows them.
- **New account** with a name and a password, or sign in with a name you already have.

The first time you sign in with Steam or Discord, the site makes an account named after you.
If that name is taken, it picks the nearest free name. Once you are signed in, you can link
your Steam account to the one you have.

## Pick your character

Choose who you are from the list. The first one is Blue, Vox's main character.

Then choose what you wear. You can pick:

- a hat,
- something on your face,
- something in your hand.

You see your choices on a turntable before you save. Press **Save character** to keep them.
Then press **Enter the Void** to go in.

The browser needs WebGPU. Current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox have it.

## Play on the desktop

On the start page, copy your launch code. Then run the desktop client with it:

```bash
vox-client --token <code>
```

The code lasts one day.

## Friends and groups

On the **Friends and raids** page you can send and answer friend requests.

- A **group** is up to 24 people who build together.
- A **raid party** is up to 8 people who take on a raid together.

## Inventory

The **Inventory** page holds your coins, deeds, portal keys, banners, trophies and wardrobe
items. You can filter by kind and by world, search, and sort. The page also lists your feats
and a ledger of every coin you have moved.
